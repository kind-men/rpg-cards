Info

Background, credits, and project notes for the RPG Cards Generator.

This project is a grateful fork of https://github.com/mathiasandresen/rpg-cards, created by Mathias Andresen. Huge thanks to Mathias and to the projects that paved the way, including https://github.com/mephitrpg/rpg-cards and the original https://github.com/crobi/rpg-cards, for making this tool possible.
